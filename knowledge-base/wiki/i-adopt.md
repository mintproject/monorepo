# I-ADOPT

**Summary**: The I-ADOPT Framework Ontology, a related effort for describing variables, into which ideas from SVO are being incorporated.

**Sources**: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-06-06

---

**I-ADOPT** is the I-ADOPT Framework Ontology (source: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf). The paper notes that ideas from the [[scientific-variables-ontology]] are being incorporated into the I-ADOPT Framework Ontology (source: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf, citing I-ADOPT 2021, https://i-adopt.github.io).

This indicates convergence between SVO and the broader community effort to standardize machine-interpretable variable descriptions for [[interoperability]].
