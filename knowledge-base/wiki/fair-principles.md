# FAIR Principles

**Summary**: Guiding principles for scientific data management — Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable — with interoperability being the focus of SVO.

**Sources**: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf, garijo-etal-escience19.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-06-06

---

**FAIR** stands for **F**indable, **A**ccessible, **I**nteroperable, **R**eusable — the FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship (source: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf, citing Wilkinson et al. 2016).

The paper highlights that the "I" — [[interoperability]] — tends to receive **less attention** than the other three letters, and positions the [[scientific-variables-ontology]] as a way to address it directly (source: Peckham_and_Stoica_2022_ICWRER_2022.pdf).

## FAIR for software

[[okg-soft|OKG-Soft]] applies FAIR to *scientific software*: exposing software descriptions on public APIs (findability), using persistent HTTP URIs (accessibility), describing software with common vocabularies/standards (interoperability), and describing inputs and outputs to ease reuse (source: garijo-etal-escience19.pdf).
